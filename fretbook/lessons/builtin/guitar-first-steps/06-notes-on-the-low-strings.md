Now move to the thicker strings. On the **G** string: **G** (open), **A** (2nd fret). On the **D** string: **D** (open), **E** (2nd fret), **F** (3rd fret). On the **A** string: **A** (open), **B** (2nd fret), **C** (3rd fret).

```fretbook:fretboard
notes: A B C D E F G
```

Play this line from the lowest note to the highest and back. Say every note name as you play it.

```fretbook:tab
G|-----------------0-2-0---------------|
D|-----------0-2-3-----3-2-0-----------|
A|---0-2-3-----------------------3-2-0-|
```

Repeat until the names come before your fingers do.
