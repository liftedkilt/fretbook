---
title: The open strings
summary: Name the six strings and play them one after another.
level: basics
order: 2
---
From the thickest string to the thinnest, a guitar in standard tuning is **E A D G B E**. A saying such as "**E**very **A**ngry **D**og **G**rowls **B**efore **E**ating" helps until the names stick. The strings are numbered the other way round: the thinnest is string 1 and the thickest is string 6.

```fretbook:fretboard
notes: E A D G B E
```

Pluck each string slowly with your thumb or a pick and say its name out loud. Then play this up-and-down pattern, one note per beat at about 60 beats per minute:

```fretbook:tab
e|-----------0-----------|
B|---------0---0---------|
G|-------0-------0-------|
D|-----0-----------0-----|
A|---0---------------0---|
E|-0-------------------0-|
```

When you can name all six strings without looking, play along in wait mode on a tab of your choice.
