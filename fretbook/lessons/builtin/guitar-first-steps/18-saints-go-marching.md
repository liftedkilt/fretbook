"When the Saints Go Marching In" is a traditional spiritual and in the public domain. In G it uses three chords: **G**, **C** and **D**.



Play one chord per bar. Each bar has four beats; strum once on each beat (or use the folk strum) and change on the "1":

```fretbook:tab
|C / / / |G / / / |D / / / |G / / / |
```

Sing the words or hum the tune while you strum. Keep the beat even.
