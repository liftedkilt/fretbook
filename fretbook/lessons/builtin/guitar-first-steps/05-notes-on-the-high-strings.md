title: Notes on the high strings
summary: Play E, F and G on the first string, and B, C and D on the second.
level: notes
order: 5
requires: [04-counting-the-beat]
---
Every fret is one half step. On the thinnest string the notes go **E** (open), **F** (1st fret), **G** (3rd fret). On the B string: **B** (open), **C** (1st fret), **D** (3rd fret).

```fretbook:fretboard
notes: E F G B C D
```

Use one finger per fret: first finger for the 1st fret, third or fourth finger for the 3rd. Practise this line up and back:

```fretbook:tab
e|---------------0-1-3-1-0---|
B|---0-1-3-1-0---------------|
```
