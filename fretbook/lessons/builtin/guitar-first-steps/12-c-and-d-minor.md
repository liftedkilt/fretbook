**C major** stretches over three frets, so keep your fingers curved. **D minor** is a small shape on the top three strings.

```fretbook:chord
name: C
```

```fretbook:chord
name: Dm
```

Strum C from the A string up, and Dm from the D string up. Change from C to Dm, then Dm to Am, four strums each. If you can play **Em Am D G C Dm E A** without looking, you know the open chords a beginner needs.
