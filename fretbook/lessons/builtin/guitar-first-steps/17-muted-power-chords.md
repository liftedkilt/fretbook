---
title: Muted power chords
summary: Stop each chord short to play a tight, rhythmic riff.
level: rhythm
order: 5
---
Rest the heel of your picking hand lightly on the strings next to the bridge. Strum the power chord: you get a dry, chopped sound. Lift the hand and the chord rings out. Switching between the two is what makes a rock riff.

```fretbook:tab
D|-2-2-2-2-2-2-2-2-|-7-7-7-7-7-7-7-7-|
A|-2-2-2-2-2-2-2-2-|-7-7-7-7-7-7-7-7-|
E|-0-0-0-0-0-0-0-0-|-5-5-5-5-5-5-5-5-|
```

Mute the first six strums and let the seventh and eighth ring. Move to the A shape in bar two and try again.
