---
title: A steady strum
summary: Down, down-up, up-down-up: a pattern for most folk songs.
level: chords
order: 2
---
Keep your strumming hand moving down and up all the time, even when it misses the strings. Count
"**1** & **2** & **3** & **4** &" and strum **D** on the numbers and **U** on the "&".

This pattern leaves out the second "up" on beat 2 and the first on beat 3:

```fretbook:tab
e|-0-----0-0---0---|
B|-0-----0-0---0---|
G|-0-----0-0---0---|
D|-2-----2-2---2---|
A|-2-----2-2---2---|
E|-0-----0-0---0---|
```

Play it on Em, then on Am. Keep the tempo slow enough that the pattern never stops.
