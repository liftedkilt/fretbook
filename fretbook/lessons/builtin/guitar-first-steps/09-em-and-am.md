**E minor** needs two fingers on the second fret. **A minor** is the same shape moved over one string, with an
extra finger on the first fret.

```fretbook:chord

```


Press just behind the fret, with the tips of your fingers, and strum every string you can see in the diagram.
Change between the two chords slowly, eight times, without stopping.
