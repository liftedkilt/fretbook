**E major** and **A major** are the two most common chords on the guitar. Neither needs a barre.

```fretbook:chord
name: E
```

```fretbook:chord
name: A
```

Strum E from the thickest string (all six); strum A without the thickest string. Press just behind the fret with the tips of your fingers, and keep your thumb behind the neck. Change between them eight times, one strum per beat.
