You now know three patterns. Pick one, count out loud, and play it on Em and Am:

1. **Four downs**: D on 1, 2, 3 and 4. Use it for slow songs.
2. **Eight eighths**: D U D U D U D U. Use it for fast songs.
3. **The folk strum**: D, D U, U D U (from the last lesson).

Muting the strings with the palm of your picking hand while you strum gives a drier, more percussive sound. Try it on pattern 2.
