---
title: Changing chords on time
summary: Move to the next chord without stopping the strum.
level: rhythm
order: 2
---
The strum never stops: if you are late for the next chord, play the strum on the strings you can hear and land the chord a beat later. Two tricks make the change on time:

- **Look ahead.** Move your fingers during the last beat of the bar, not after the downbeat.
- **Keep common fingers.** C to Am keeps the first and second fingers where they are. Em to Am moves the same fingers one string.

```fretbook:tab
e|-0-0-0-0-|-0-0-0-0-|
B|-1-1-1-1-|-1-1-1-1-|
G|-0-0-0-0-|-2-2-2-2-|
D|-2-2-2-2-|-2-2-2-2-|
A|-3-3-3-3-|-0-0-0-0-|
E|---------|---------|
```

Play it slowly, four strums on C and four on Am, then repeat. Speed up by 5 bpm only when you made ten clean changes in a row.
