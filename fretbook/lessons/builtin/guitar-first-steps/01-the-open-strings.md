From the thickest string to the thinnest, a guitar in standard tuning is **E A D G B E**. A saying such as
"**E**very **A**ngry **D**og **G**rowls **B**efore **E**ating" helps until the names stick.

Here are the open strings on the neck. Pluck each one slowly and say its name out loud.

```fretbook:fretboard
notes: E A D G B E
```

Check your tuning with **Tools → Tuner** before you start: everything else in this course sounds wrong on a guitar
that is out of tune.

When you can name all six strings without looking, mark this lesson as done.
