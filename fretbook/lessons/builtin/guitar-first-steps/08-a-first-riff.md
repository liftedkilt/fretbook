A **riff** is a short pattern that repeats. This one is original to Fretbook and uses only the open **E** string and the 2nd, 3rd and 5th fret of the **A** string. Play every note with a down-stroke of the pick (or your thumb) and let the open strings ring.

```fretbook:tab
A|---------2-3---------2-3-5-|
E|-0-0-0-0-----0-0-0-0-------|
```

Count "1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &" and keep your picking hand moving. Loop the riff until you can play it four times in a row without a mistake.
