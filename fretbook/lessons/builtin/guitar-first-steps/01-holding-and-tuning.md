---
title: Holding and tuning
summary: Sit comfortably, hold the guitar, and get it in tune.
level: basics
order: 1
---
Sit up straight with the guitar's body resting on your right thigh (left, if you play left-handed) and the neck tilted slightly upward. Your fretting hand holds the neck like a handshake, with the thumb behind it; your other forearm rests on the body so the hand can swing freely over the strings.

Tune before every session. Open **Tools → Tuner**, pluck one string at a time and turn its tuning peg until the needle sits in the middle. Go from the thickest string to the thinnest: **E A D G B E**. Tune up to a note rather than down to it, so the string stays put.

```fretbook:fretboard
notes: E A D G B E
```

When all six strings read in tune, play the generated warm-up below slowly. It also checks that you can fret and pluck cleanly.

```fretbook:exercise
preset: spiderWarmUp
title: Finger warm-up
```

type: wait
