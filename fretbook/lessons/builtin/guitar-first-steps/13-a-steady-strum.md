---
title: A steady strum
summary: Down, down-up, up-down-up: a pattern for most folk songs.
level: rhythm
order: 1
---
Keep your strumming hand moving down and up all the time, even when it misses the strings. Count "**1** & **2** & **3** & **4** &" and strum **D** on the numbers and **U** on the "&".

This pattern leaves out some of the "ups": down on 1 and 2, up on the "&" of 2, up on the "&" of 3, down on 4, up on the "&" of 4:

```fretbook:tab
e|-0---0-0---0-0-|
B|-0---0-0---0-0-|
G|-0---0-0---0-0-|
D|-2---2-2---2-2-|
A|-2---2-2---2-2-|
E|-0---0-0---0-0-|
  1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &
```

Play it on Em, then on Am. Keep the tempo slow enough that the pattern never stops.
