summary: Two more open chords, so you can change between E, A, D and G.

**D major** uses the four thinnest strings: skip the two thickest. **G major** uses all six.

```fretbook:chord
name: D
```

```fretbook:chord
name: G
```

Play D then G, four strums each, and count out loud so the change comes on the beat. If you are late, slow down. Then try A to D and E to A, two of the most common changes in popular music.
