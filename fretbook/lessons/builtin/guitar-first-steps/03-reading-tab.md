**Tablature** (tab) shows where to put your fingers. Each line is a string, with the thinnest string at the top, and each number is a fret: `0` is an open string, `1` is the first fret, and so on. Notes in a column are played together; notes left to right are played one after another.

```fretbook:tab
e|---------------0---|
B|-----------1-------|
G|-------2-----------|
D|---3---------------|
A|-------------------|
E|-------------------|
```

Read it aloud: third fret of the D string, then second fret of the G string, then first fret of B, then open high E. Play it four times, slowly.

Fretbook shows tab for every song in your library. Open any song and follow the cursor along the notes.
