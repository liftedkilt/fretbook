A **power chord** is a root and its fifth. It has no third, so it is neither major nor minor, and it sounds big when you add distortion. Use your first finger on the root and your third finger two frets higher on the next string.

```fretbook:chord
name: E5
```

```fretbook:chord
name: A5
```

```fretbook:chord
name: D5
```

```fretbook:tab
D|-2---7---|
A|-2---7---|
E|-0---5---|
  E5  A5
```

Move the shape along the neck: the root is whichever note your first finger is on. Strum only the two or three strings of the shape and mute the others with the side of your hand.
