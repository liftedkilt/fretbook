A **walking bass line** plays one note per beat and moves in small steps from one chord to the next. This one is for a bar of A (on the A string) and a bar of D (on the D string); it climbs three frets and steps back:

```fretbook:tab
D|---------|-0-2-3-2-|
A|-0-2-3-2-|---------|
```

Play one note per beat and aim the last note of each bar at the next root.
