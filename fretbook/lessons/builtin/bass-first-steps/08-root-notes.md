The **root** is the note a chord is named after. For an **A** chord play A; for an **E** chord play E. A bass line that plays only the roots, one on every beat, already holds the song together.

```fretbook:tab
E|-0-0-0-0-|-5-5-5-5-|-7-7-7-7-|-5-5-5-5-|
   E         A         B         A
```

Play it with the metronome at 70.
