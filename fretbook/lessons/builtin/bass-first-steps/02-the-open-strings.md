title: The open strings
summary: Name the four strings and find them on the neck.
level: basics
order: 2
---
A four-string bass in standard tuning is **E A D G**, from the thickest string to the thinnest. They are the same notes as the four lowest strings of a guitar, one octave lower.

```fretbook:fretboard
notes: E A D G
instrument: bass
```

Pluck each string with your first two fingers, one after the other, and say each name. Keep the plucking fingers resting on the string below the one you play.

```fretbook:tab
G|-----------0-|
D|-------0-----|
A|---0---------|
E|-0-----------|
```

Check the tuning with **Tools → Tuner** first.
