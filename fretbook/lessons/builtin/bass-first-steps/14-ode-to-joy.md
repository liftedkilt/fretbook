"Ode to Joy" (Ludwig van Beethoven, 1824) is in the public domain. On bass it lies under your fingers on the D and G strings, in the key of C. The G is the open string, the other notes are on the D string (open, 2nd and 3rd fret) and the 3rd fret of the A string.

```fretbook:tab
G|------0-|0-------|--------|--------|
D|2-2-3---|--3-2-0-|----0-2-|2-0-0---|
A|--------|--------|3-3-----|--------|
E|--------|--------|--------|--------|
```

Count four beats a bar and keep the notes even. Use the metronome at 60.
