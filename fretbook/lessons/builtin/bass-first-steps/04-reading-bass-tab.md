Bass tab has **four lines**, one for each string, with the thickest at the bottom (E A D G, from the bottom up). The numbers are frets: `0` is open, `3` is the third fret.

```fretbook:tab
G|-------------0-|
D|-----------2---|
A|-------3-------|
E|-0-3-----------|
```

Read it aloud: open E, third fret on E, third fret on A, second fret on D, open G. Fretbook shows tab for every song in your library. Open any bass tab and follow the cursor.
