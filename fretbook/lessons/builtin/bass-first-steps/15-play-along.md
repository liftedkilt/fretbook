summary: Hold a real song together with roots and fifths.

Pick a slow song from your library. Find its chords, play the root of each chord on every beat, and add the fifth when you feel at ease. Use the speed trainer from 60 % up and loop the part you like best.

When you can play the whole song once with the click, mark this lesson as done.
