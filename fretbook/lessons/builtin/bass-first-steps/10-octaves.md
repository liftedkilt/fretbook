title: Octaves
summary: Jump up two strings and two frets for the same note, higher.

order: 3
---
The **octave** of a note sits two strings higher and two frets up: E on the open E string, then E on the 2nd fret of the D string. Playing the root, then the octave, gives a bouncy, disco-like line.

```fretbook:tab
D|---2---2---|---7---7---|
A|-----------|-----------|
E|-0---0-----|-5---5-----|
```

Keep the fretting finger and the thumb relaxed on the jump.
