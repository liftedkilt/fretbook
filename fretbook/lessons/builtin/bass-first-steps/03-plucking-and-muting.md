---
title: Plucking and muting
summary: Alternate two fingers and stop the notes you do not want.
level: basics
order: 3
---
Pluck with your index and middle fingers **in turn**: 1-2-1-2. Never use the same finger twice in a row; it makes the rhythm uneven. After you pluck, rest the finger on the next string down, so the string you left rings only as long as you want.

With your fretting hand, lift a finger a little to stop a note. With the side of the plucking hand, rest on the strings above so they do not ring.

```fretbook:tab
E|-0-0-0-0-0-0-0-0-|
  1 2 1 2 1 2 1 2
```

Play it at 60 bpm and listen for equal volume from both fingers.
