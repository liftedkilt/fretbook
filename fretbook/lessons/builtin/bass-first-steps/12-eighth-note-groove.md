summary: Eight notes to a bar, with accents on the beat.

Play the root eight times in a bar, with a little more weight on the beats than on the "&"s. This is the sound under most rock songs.

```fretbook:tab
E|-0-0-0-0-0-0-0-0-|-5-5-5-5-5-5-5-5-|
   1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &
```

Use alternate plucking, and keep the gaps between the notes equal.
