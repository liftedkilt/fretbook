title: Lock in with the beat
summary: Play every note exactly on the click.
level: groove
order: 1
---
A bass player's main job is to land together with the drums. Set the metronome in **Tools → Metronome** to 70 and play one root note on every click. Then play two notes per click, then four.

The notes are only the same length when you stop them on purpose: lift the fretting finger a little at the end of each note so each one is short and tidy.
