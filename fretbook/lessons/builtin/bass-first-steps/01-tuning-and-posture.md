---
title: Tuning and posture
summary: Sit or stand comfortably, and get the strings in tune.
level: basics
order: 1
---
Hold the bass so that your fretting arm hangs relaxed and the neck points slightly upward. Sit with the body on your thigh or use a strap long enough to bring the bass to the same place as when you sit. Your thumb rests behind the neck.

Open **Tools → Tuner** and tune from the thickest string to the thinnest: **E A D G**. Tune up to the note.
