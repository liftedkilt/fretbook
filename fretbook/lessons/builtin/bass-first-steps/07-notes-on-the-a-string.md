On the A string: **A** (open), **B** (2nd fret), **C** (3rd fret) and **D** (5th fret). Move between the E and A strings with the same finger shape.

```fretbook:fretboard
notes: A B C D
instrument: bass
```

```fretbook:tab
G|---------------|
D|---------------|
A|-0-2-3-5-3-2-0-|
E|---------------|
```

Slide your hand, not just your finger, when you cross to a new string.
